## Complete PC Keyboard and Mouse Mapping Guide

Playing third-person soulslike titles on mouse and keyboard can feel intimidating, but an optimized setup gives you distinct advantages in camera control and immediate skill access. In **False Hero PC**, your mask lets you harvest arts from fallen adversaries, requiring quick finger placement across primary attacks and assigned skill slots. 

The default keybindings provide a solid starting point, but competitive player experience suggests moving certain defensive actions off standard modifiers to minimize finger travel time. 

The table below outlines both the baseline default keybindings and an optimized layout tailored for faster combo execution:

| Action Category | Default Keybind | Optimized Community Layout | Mechanical Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Light Attack / Primary Strike** | Left Mouse Button | Left Mouse Button | Standard fast weapon combo |
| **Heavy Attack / Power Strike** | Shift + LMB | Mouse Button 4 (Thumb) | High-stagger windup strike |
| **Evade / Dash Roll** | Spacebar | Spacebar | Invulnerability frame (i-frame) dodge |

| **Use Consumable / Flask** | R / C | C | Quick-healing without halting movement |
| **Camera Reset** | Mouse Movement | Mouse Movement | Free-form environmental observation |

### Key Remapping Strategies for Keyboard Players

Placing your secondary attack or active Combat Art on side mouse buttons (Mouse 4 and Mouse 5) eliminates awkward finger contortions while maintaining full directional WASD movement. Player experience highlights that pressing `Q` or `E` while simultaneously strafing diagonally (`W` + `A` or `W` + `D`) can stall your dodge rhythm during frantic multi-phase boss encounters.

Additionally, consider binding your primary dodge/dash exclusively to `Spacebar` and unlinking tap-sprint functions if you experience accidental rolls when attempting to sprint out of wide area-of-effect hazards.

### Controller Tuning and Deadzone Management

To get the most responsive feel when running **False Hero PC** through Steam Input, adjust your controller deadzones within the Steam overlay. Setting your inner stick deadzone to roughly 5% to 8% prevents unwanted stick drift while delivering immediate, instantaneous reaction times when evading boss combos.

Community reports suggest mapping Dodge/Roll to an underside paddle (such as L4 or R4 on the Steam Deck or an Xbox Elite controller). Doing so allows you to sprint, dodge, and aim the camera simultaneously without ever lifting your thumb off the right analog stick.

## Combat Arts and Input Mechanics: Timing Your Combos

Combat in the corrupted realm centers on a high-tempo feedback loop: learning enemy movement patterns, securing the kill, harvesting their signature move with your scholar's mask, and instantly integrating that attack into your offensive rotation.

Understanding input buffering and cancel windows in **False Hero PC** is vital to surviving against fast-moving bosses. The combat engine does not encourage thoughtless button mashing; rather, it uses an intentional action queue.

The table below outlines how common input chains interact within the game engine:

| Action Sequence | Execution Window | Engine Response / Buffer Rules | Recommended Strategic Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Light Strike > Stolen Art** | Mid-swing animation | Buffers the Art to trigger on the earliest recovery frame | Quick burst damage before boss retaliates |
| **Heavy Strike > Dodge Cancel** | Before apex of windup | Cancels attack commitment with full i-frame roll | Baiting boss swings safely |
| **Evade > Counter Strike** | Immediately upon i-frame exit | Triggers dedicated lunging thrust with bonus stagger | Closing distance following a boss miss |
| **Double Art Chain** | Trigger Art 2 during Art 1 hitstop | Chains two enemy skills with zero neutral animation | Depleting poise meters during stagger windows |
| **God Power Burst** | Neutral stance or hit recovery | Overrides standard hitstun with invulnerable startup | Emergency defense or finishing off a boss phase |

### Managing Input Buffering and Animation Locks

If you hit your attack button three times rapidly, the engine will queue all three swings. If an enemy winds up a lethal overhead strike during your first swing, an over-buffered input will prevent you from rolling in time. 

Train your fingers to press inputs deliberately—one tap per intended action. Community feedback frequently notes that mastering this discipline separates players who breeze through the mountaintop kingdom of Wisdom from those stuck on introductory bosses.

## Advanced Combat Tactics and Camera Controls

Surviving the surreal worlds of lush swamps, chilling ruins, and ancient cities requires mastering the game's camera behavior alongside your weapon moveset. Many soulslike players struggle when engaging massive bosses because large enemy models can obscure attack telegraphs when locked on at close range.

### Mastering Lock-On Flexibility

The lock-on feature automatically centers your target, but keeping it active at all times can work against you. In encounters featuring swarming minions or multi-part bosses like the giant arachnoid, playing unlocked grants superior situational awareness. 

- **Lock On:** Ideal for one-on-one humanoid duels, flying angelic adversaries, and landing direct-line projectile Combat Arts.
- **Lock Off:** Essential when navigating wide crowd-control encounters, running away from sweeping ground-hazard explosions, or manually steering wide horizontal heavy slashes.

### Positioning, Poise, and Recovery Frames

Because the title eschews traditional gear grinding in favor of pure skill mastery, your defensive positioning is your primary shield. When chaining stolen moves, evaluate each art's recovery time:
- Fast slashes (derived from basic sword-wielding husks) carry brief recovery windows, making them safe combo starters.
- Crushing beast slams deal massive stagger damage but leave you vulnerable to counter-attacks if mistimed.

Always reserve at least 15% to 20% of your stamina bar to execute a reactive dodge roll immediately after your final offensive input.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you play False Hero PC effectively on keyboard and mouse?
Yes. While many players naturally gravitate toward controllers for soulslike games, **False Hero PC** offers full keyboard and mouse functionality. By binding secondary attacks and active Combat Arts to auxiliary side thumb buttons on your mouse, you can achieve swift, precise camera adjustments and execute combos just as effectively as gamepad users.

### Does False Hero PC feature native controller support on Steam?
Yes, the title natively supports both Xbox and PlayStation controllers with proper in-game prompts. Additionally, because the game is rated Playable on Steam Deck, you can seamlessly remap your inputs using Steam Input profiles or community layouts to best fit your personal grip preference.

### How do you steal and trigger enemy abilities in False Hero PC?
Your character's mask allows you to steal unique abilities from slain enemies. Once an enemy is defeated, interact with their remains to claim their combat art. You can equip multiple arts in your loadout slots and activate them in real time using your assigned skill hotkeys (`Q`/`R` on keyboard, or `LB`/`LT` on controller) to weave them directly into your regular sword combos.

### How can I reduce input delay while playing False Hero PC?
To minimize input lag, run the game in Exclusive Fullscreen mode, turn off in-game V-Sync, and set an external frame rate cap just below your monitor's maximum refresh rate via your graphics control panel. If playing on a gamepad, lowering the analog stick deadzones in Steam's controller configuration will make your movement and dodging feel noticeably more responsive.
