## Advanced Combat Tactics for Surviving Group Encounters

Handling multiple enemies simultaneously is among the steepest learning curves in the game. In tight corridors and treacherous swamp waters, camera positioning and environmental hazards present as much danger as the beasts themselves.

First, always leverage environmental chokepoints. Forcing swarming creatures through narrow archways or bridges invalidates their flanking maneuvers, turning a chaotic multi-angle skirmish into an orderly linear fight.

Second, pay attention to friendly fire mechanics. Massive mechanical golems and raging draconic beasts frequently damage their own allied minions during sweeping cleaves. Positioning smaller fodder between yourself and an attacking giant allows the larger creature's strike to clear the arena for you.

Community reports emphasize the value of baiting heavy attacks, triggering enemy friendly-fire, and immediately activating invulnerability-frame arts. Perfecting this rhythm turns aggressive enemy swarms into manageable, self-destructive assets.
