## Frequently Asked Questions About False Hero Steal Attacks

### How do you consistently trigger false hero steal attacks without getting interrupted?
To execute **false hero steal attacks** safely, always eliminate surrounding minions before channeling the siphon on an elite enemy. Alternatively, you can use crowd-control skills like stuns or freezes to create a safe 2-second extraction pocket.

### Do stolen attacks stay permanently in your inventory?
In *False Hero*, regular enemy abilities remain in your active slots until you manually replace them or exhaust their total charges. Boss skills, once unlocked, can be permanently re-equipped at resting shrines, allowing you to tailor your loadout before tackling new areas.

### Can you upgrade false hero steal attacks to deal more damage?
Yes. Stolen abilities inherit damage bonuses from your core character attributes (Strength, Dexterity, and Attunement) as well as global siphon relics discovered in hidden chests throughout the game world.

### What happens if I attempt to steal an attack while my slots are full?
If your ability bar is full when you trigger the siphon, a quick-swap prompt appears on your HUD. This lets you choose which active skill to overwrite or cancel the action entirely without expending your Spirit Charges.
