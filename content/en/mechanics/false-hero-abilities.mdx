*   **Utilize Reset Windows:** After staggering a boss, execute your highest-cost divine or death ability before landing the final critical strike.
*   **Revisit Explored Zones:** Return to earlier areas with new traversal abilities to access secluded crypts containing hidden combat arts scrolls.

By prioritizing tactical adaptability over mindless grinding, you will steadily dismantle every horror the Land of the Gods throws your way. Experimenting with unconventional skill pairings often reveals game-changing synergies that make short work of even the most punishing encounters.

## Frequently Asked Questions About False Hero Abilities

### How do you unlock new false hero abilities?
You unlock new **false hero abilities** primarily by defeating enemies and absorbing their combat arts using your mask. Elite monsters, optional mini-bosses, and main storyline bosses each yield distinct attacks that can be equipped directly to your custom combo slots. Additional divine arts can be discovered in ancient shrines and hidden puzzle vaults scattered across the world.

### Can you equip multiple combat arts at the same time?
Yes. The combat system lets you equip several combat arts simultaneously across various combo strings and active trigger inputs. This allows you to combine rapid melee thrusts, heavy guard-breaking slams, and ranged divine projectiles into a single continuous chain of attacks.

### Are false hero abilities permanently lost if you die?
No. Once your mask absorbs a combat art, it is permanently added to your library of moves. Dying causes you to respawn at the nearest checkpoint, but you retain all unlocked skills and scrolls, letting you adapt your loadout immediately without losing your acquired mechanics.

### Do bosses drop unique abilities that regular enemies do not possess?
Yes. Major bosses—such as ancient dragons, mechanical colossi, and angelic guardians—provide powerful signature arts. These boss-tier skills feature unique visual animations, larger areas of effect, and specialized mechanics, giving you potent options for high-difficulty encounters later in the game.
