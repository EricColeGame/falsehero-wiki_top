## How Skill Acquisition Works in False Hero

In *False Hero*, progression is entirely performance-driven. Rather than spending currency at rest sites to raise arbitrary statistics like Dexterity or Strength, your offensive toolkit expands dynamically through the mask's absorption power. Whenever you defeat an enemy possessing a signature combat maneuver, your mask captures their technique, transforming it into an equipable Combat Art.

According to community reports and early player testing, abilities are not simply passive upgrades. Each stolen skill is an active combat input that consumes stamina or deity focus, slotting seamlessly into your standard light and heavy attack strings. 

```
[Enemy Defeat] ➔ [Mask Absorption] ➔ [Codex Unlock] ➔ [Active Skill Slotting] ➔ [Combo Weaving]
```

To maximize your efficiency when building a moveset, keep these core acquisition rules in mind:

*   **Defeat Requirement:** You must land the killing blow or finish an enemy during a stagger state to register their attack into your mask's library.
*   **Variant Rarities:** Elite enemies and wandering minibosses drop refined, high-impact variants of basic monster strikes with wider hitboxes or secondary elemental properties.
*   **Slot Constraints:** You possess a restricted number of active skill slots, requiring deliberate choices between defensive counters, gap-closers, and high-damage finishers.

## Progression Tips: Mastering Skills Without Traditional Grinding

Without levels to grind or gear score to inflate, your mastery over **false hero skills** represents your true power level. Overcoming challenging roadblocks requires learning enemy tells, identifying which monsters hold the counter-skills for upcoming bosses, and refining execution speed.

To streamline your progression through the corrupted kingdoms, utilize these strategic practices:

*   **Hunt Optional Elites:** Wandering mini-bosses guarding dead-end ruins consistently possess superior variants of basic skills. Defeating them early provides combat arts that punch far above your current zone tier.
*   **Study Telegraph Windows:** Many stolen attacks inherit the exact startup frames of the original monster. If a beast took two seconds to wind up an attack, your character will too. Reserve slow arts for downed or stunned targets.
*   **Tailor Loadouts to Boss Arenas:** Broad horizontal sweeps are devastating in open fields but bounce off narrow cave walls. Swap to vertical thrusts and overhead slams when fighting in cramped dungeon corridors.
*   **Track Skill Mastery Milestones:** Certain skills evolve with repeated, successful execution. Landing critical hits or perfect counters with specific arts can unlock reduced stamina costs or faster animation recoveries.

## Frequently Asked Questions About False Hero Skills

### Can I permanently miss any false hero skills during a playthrough?
Most standard combat arts can be acquired at any time since regular enemies respawn when you rest or re-enter a biome. However, certain unique boss-specific combat arts require you to choose specific narrative pathways or defeat optional elite encounters before advancing the world state. If you align fully with Death or the Gods, certain faction-exclusive skills may become locked until New Game Plus.

### How many false hero skills can I equip at the same time?
Your scholar's mask features a limited set of active memory slots. Typically, you can equip up to three active stolen combat arts alongside one dedicated Divine God Power and your core weapon moveset. Expanding your mask's capacity involves locating hidden Scholar Relics scattered throughout hidden chambers in the game's interconnected world.

### Do false hero skills scale with character stats?
No. *False Hero* does not use an attribute-based scaling system (such as Strength or Dexterity scaling). Instead, your skill effectiveness scales through your weapon's base reinforcement, the execution timing of your combos, and mastery bonuses unlocked by landing clean hits with specific arts during combat.

### Can stolen enemy skills be interrupted by incoming attacks?
Yes. Stolen combat arts do not grant automatic invulnerability or limitless poise. If an enemy hits you during a skill's startup animation, your attack can be deflected or canceled entirely, costing you the invested stamina. For slow, heavy abilities, ensure the target is recovering from a miss or locked in hitstun before initiating the art.
