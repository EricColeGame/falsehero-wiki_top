## Core Gameplay: The Mask Mechanic and Ability Absorption

What separates the **False Hero demo** from traditional Soulslike clones is its signature mask ability. When you defeat an enemy, your mask absorbs their combat essence, unlocking their signature attack as an assignable combat art.

This mechanic eliminates standard class restrictions. Instead of being locked into a rigid mage, rogue, or warrior archetype, players build custom combo strings. You can open an engagement with an agile leap stolen from a skirmisher, transition into a heavy cleave taken from an armored warden, and finish with a ranged blast acquired from an eldritch cultist.

Fluid evasion sits at the center of survival. The dodge roll features tight invulnerability windows, forcing you to read attack wind-ups rather than spam defensive inputs. Weaving absorbed attacks into basic sword slashes builds momentum, opening defensive gaps in heavily armored targets.

## Tips for Conquering the Preview Chapter

To get the most out of your playthrough, keep these essential tactics in mind:

- **Do Not Neglect Deflections:** Dodging consumes stamina, but learning parry timings preserves your positioning and sets up devastating counter-strikes.
- **Cycle Stolen Moves Regularly:** Different enemy types exhibit specific elemental and posture weaknesses. If a blunt skill struggles against nimble targets, swap to a rapid piercing move.
- **Watch Environmental Edges:** The Kingdom of Wisdom features perilous vertical cliffs. Position yourself so enemy knockbacks send foes off edges rather than dropping you into the abyss.
- **Study Telegraph Wind-ups:** Bosses deliberately delay their downward swings. Avoid panic-rolling early; wait for the visual snap before hitting dodge.

Taking the time to master these fundamentals makes the initial chapter an engaging, highly replayable challenge.
