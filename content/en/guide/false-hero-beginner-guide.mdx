## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I focus on first in this false hero beginner guide?
During your first few days, prioritize clocking into work during the Early Morning slot. Earning money early ensures you can afford cafe visits and gift purchases, which are required to unlock the twins' house. Once your income is steady, begin alternating your noon hours between Jane's office therapy sessions and Melanie's house.

### Why won't Tiffany talk to me or trigger new scenes?
Tiffany's storyline is entirely dependent on your progress with Melanie. If Tiffany is not responding or events fail to load, return to Melanie's bedroom at noon. You must complete Melanie's check-ups and give her advice regarding Tiffany (Phases 1 through 4) before Tiffany's individual evening and night scenes become available.

### How do I trigger Lucy's bathroom events if the door is locked?
Lucy's bathroom scenes require you to complete her preceding storyline milestones first. Ensure you have played the late-night console sessions with her in the bedroom and talked to her in the kitchen during the early morning. Lucy's six bathroom events unlock sequentially; missing an earlier domestic trigger prevents the next one from appearing.

### Can I follow this false hero beginner guide to romance all characters in a single run?
Yes. The scheduling system allows you to balance storylines across different daily time slots without permanent locks. By reserving your early mornings for work, noons for Jane or Melanie, afternoons for the cafe, and nights for Lucy or the twins, you can fully explore all character storylines in a single playthrough.
