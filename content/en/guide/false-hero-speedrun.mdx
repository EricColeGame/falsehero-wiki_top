## Core Movement Mechanics and Time-Saving Tech

Every successful **false hero speedrun** relies on converting standard character movement into forward momentum. Passive traversal burns valuable seconds, making animation cancels and collision exploitation essential skills.

### 1. Dash-Cancel Velocity Buffering
By buffering a primary attack into the recovery frames of a forward dash, your character retains horizontal momentum without enduring the deceleration penalty. When chained along flat pathways, dash-canceling yields an estimated 14% speed boost over standard sprint cycles.

### 2. Edge Slides and Ledge Snapping
Jumping immediately before rolling over a drop allows your model to bypass fall-stagger animations. Landing directly onto geometry edges snaps the character into a standing state, resetting your jump buffer instantly.

### 3. Damage Boosting
While risky in low-health scenarios, deliberate damage boosting off minor environmental hazards or low-damage projectiles lets runners bypass complex platforming cycles. Player experience indicates that taking intentional knockback through laser grids can shave off three to four seconds of cycling wait times.

## Boss Strategies: Conquering Ignorance and Advanced Protection Unit

Boss fights represent the largest potential time swings in any attempt. In a competitive **false hero speedrun**, treating boss encounters as survival encounters will destroy your split times; you must play with high-risk offensive pressure to force boss AI loops.

### Ignorance: The Stagger-Lock Strategy
The boss *Ignorance* features wide sweeping strikes and an area-of-effect slam. The key to finishing this fight in roughly two minutes (matching the top standard of 2m 05s set by Taliron) is baiting the primary horizontal slash, parrying on frame three, and punishing with charged heavy attacks. 

*   **Phase 1:** Close the distance instantly. Force Ignorance toward the right wall arena barrier. Pinning the boss limits its retreat animations.
*   **Phase 2:** At 50% health, Ignorance charges an arena-wide shockwave. Do not retreat. Jump through the inner ring and deliver falling strikes to break its posture before the wave finishes expanding.

### Advanced Protection Unit: Shield Stripping & Burst Cycles
The *Advanced Protection Unit* requires an entirely different approach. With high armor values and tracking ordnance, standard melee trades result in massive time loss.

*   **Armor Burn:** Open the fight by sticking magnetic explosives or high-impact projectiles directly to the central chassis.
*   **Turret Cycling:** When the unit summons peripheral drone supports, ignore the adds unless they overlap with the boss's hitbox. Funnel all splash damage directly into the core to skip the secondary repair phase entirely.

## Practicing Efficiently and Avoiding Reset Fatigue

Speedrunning is as much a mental game as a technical one. Many newcomers to the **false hero speedrun** community fall into the trap of resetting on the very first mistake in Sector 1. While restarting is necessary when chasing world records, early resets rob you of late-game practice.

Develop a partitioned practice routine:
1.  **Warm-up Block (15 mins):** Practice dash-canceling corridors and ledge-snapping execution.
2.  **Boss Gauntlet (30 mins):** Load dedicated save files directly before *Ignorance* and the *Advanced Protection Unit* to practice damage loops.
3.  **Full Runs (60–90 mins):** Run full Any% sets regardless of early mistakes, learning how to recover lost pace when things go wrong.

By keeping your technical foundation sharp and approaching splits methodically, you will consistently drop seconds and establish your name on the leaderboards.

## Frequently Asked Questions About False Hero Speedruns

### What is the most important skill for a beginner in a false hero speedrun?
Mastering dash-canceling and movement buffers is far more critical than learning risky boss skips. Clean, consistent movement through hallways accounts for the majority of time saved across an entire run.

### Is controller or keyboard-and-mouse preferred for high-level runs?
Both input methods are viable, but many top players prefer keyboard-and-mouse for faster directional snapping and menu navigation. Controller analog sticks, however, provide smoother angles during specific platforming sections.

### How do I handle bad RNG during the Advanced Protection Unit encounter?
If the boss initiates an extended aerial bombardment pattern, switch immediately to defensive projectile spam rather than waiting on the ground. Staying active ensures your posture-meter buildup doesn't decay while you wait for the boss to re-enter melee range.

### Do cutscenes count against my total time in Any% runs?
Official leaderboard timing typically relies on Real Time Attack (RTA) or Loadless Time depending on the integrated autosplitter. Cutscene skips are mandatory in competitive play because unskippable dialogue sequences will directly add to your final RTA submission.
