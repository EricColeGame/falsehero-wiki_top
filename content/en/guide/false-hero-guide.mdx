## Key Gameplay Tips and Progression Traps to Avoid

Navigating complex branching flags requires discipline. Many players run into progression stalls because an uncompleted trigger from one character blocks time slots required by another.

* **Maintain Staggered Saves**: Visual novels with dense conditional logic can soft-lock a specific branch if you sleep through a required window. Save at the start of every in-game week.
* **Prioritize Early Morning Income**: You need cash to buy gifts for the twins at the cafe. Use your early mornings to work until your treasury has a healthy buffer.
* **Focus One Secondary Path at a Time**: While Lucy can be pursued naturally alongside your daily routine at home, attempting to advance Jane and the Twins concurrently during the noon slot will slow down both routes. Choose one venue per game week.
* **Observe Dialogue Flags**: During cafe and domestic events, always pick supportive, forward-moving dialogue. Backtracking or defensive choices can postpone route progression.

## Frequently Asked Questions About False Hero

### Why won't Tiffany talk to me in the living room?
As detailed in this **false hero guide**, Tiffany's events are entirely dependent on Melanie's progression. You must visit Melanie's room at noon and complete the "Advices to Tiffany" (stages 1 through 4) before Tiffany's evening living room scenes will trigger.

### How do I unlock the twins' house?
Visit the cafe during the afternoon to buy items generously, then repeatedly order coffee and select the "Spy" option at noon. Continuing this loop will eventually unlock their home address on your map.

### Can I pursue Lucy and Jane at the same time?
Yes. Lucy’s primary progression points take place during the early morning, morning, night, and late-night slots at the house, whereas Jane's interactions occur exclusively at the workplace during the noon hour. Their daily schedules do not conflict directly.

### What should I do if a character interaction is missing from a room?
If a character is not present at the listed time slot, verify that you completed their preceding milestone. Visual novels often require advancing to the next in-game calendar day for the game's script engine to register that a flag has been cleared.
