## Lucy Storyline Progression and Event Checklist

Lucy’s route combines living-room hobby interactions, intimate late-night conversations, and recurring morning events. Her progression requires strict dedication to the late-night and morning slots at your residence.

Community reports show that Lucy's route serves as a comfortable starting line because her triggers rarely conflict with early-game work shifts. Keep a dedicated save file right before initiating the "Night Games" sequence to explore alternate dialogue outcomes freely.

```
Living Room (Night) ➔ Bedroom (Late Night) ➔ Kitchen (Early Morning) ➔ Bathroom (Morning) ➔ Living Room (Noon)
```

### Step-by-Step Lucy Route

1. **Console Gaming Introduction:** Head to the Living Room during the **Night** slot. Select the Xbox console to interact with Lucy.
2. **Night Games (1/5 to 5/5):** Visit the Bedroom during the **Late Night** slot. You must complete five separate late-night sessions across consecutive days to trigger her full affinity path.
3. **Breakfast Dialogue:** Following early bedroom milestones, visit the Kitchen during **Early Morning** to speak with her directly.
4. **Bathroom Sequence (1/6 to 6/6):** Enter the Bathroom during the **Morning** slot. This initiates a sequential, multi-part event chain consisting of six distinct interactions.
5. **Rehearsal Sessions:** Once bathroom events reach mid-tier progress, head to the Living Room at **Noon** to assist with her rehearsals.

| Event Name | Location | Required Time | Prerequisites / Sequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Night Games 1–5** | Bedroom | Late Night | Completed previous tier; requires 5 separate nights |
| **Kitchen Check-in** | Kitchen | Early Morning | Active Night Games progress |
| **Bathroom Events 1–6** | Bathroom | Morning | Triggers in numerical order across 6 distinct mornings |

As outlined in this **false hero walkthrough**, scheduling is everything: ensure you do not stay up late at the Twins' house when Lucy's morning bathroom milestones are active.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you romance multiple heroines in a single playthrough?
Yes. The narrative allows concurrent character routes, provided your daily schedule does not create timing conflicts. For instance, Jane's noon office therapy sessions can easily be pursued alongside Lucy's late-night bedroom interactions.

### Why is a step-by-step false hero walkthrough necessary for unlocking Tiffany?
Tiffany lacks independent map pins during early progression chapters. Her scenes, dialogue prompts, and personal events trigger exclusively through milestones achieved inside Melanie's bedroom and living room. Following an exact event checklist prevents players from stalling her affinity development.

### How many times do I need to spy at the café to unlock the Twins' house?
Community reports indicate you must visit the Café at Noon, buy coffee, and spy between two and four times. Ensure you choose generous answers during afternoon gift scenes to trigger the house invite prompt as early as possible.

### What should I do if an event will not trigger at the specified time?
Verify whether an active prerequisite chain remains unresolved. For example, Lucy’s morning bathroom events require active progress in her late-night bedroom games. If you meet all criteria and an icon still does not show, advance the calendar by one full day while checking work attendance status.
