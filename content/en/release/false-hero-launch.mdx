## Core Combat Loop: How the Ability-Stealing System Works

Unlike traditional soulslike games where weapon movesets remain static until you visit a resting checkpoint or upgrade vendor, *False Hero* empowers you to strip attacks straight from dying adversaries mid-clash. 

When an enemy is staggered or falls below an execution threshold, a timed grapple trigger allows you to harvest their core combat essence. This absorbed attack slots dynamically into your active skill wheel, allowing you to link standard light and heavy blade swings into devastating foreign techniques.

```
Standard Blade Slash -> Enemy Stagger -> Execution/Siphon -> Instant Spell/Thrust Cancel -> Finisher
```

Community reports highlight that the transition between your baseline melee weapon and stolen divine skills is seamless, carrying zero activation lag when canceled out of a standard dodge roll.

## Essential Starter Strategies for Surviving the Early Game

Jumping into the Land of the Gods without a plan will result in swift defeat. Early zones punish reckless button mashing, as stamina exhaustion leaves you completely defenseless against tracking attacks. To hit the ground running after the **false hero launch**, adopting systematic combat habits is vital.

### 1. Master the Siphon Window
Do not wait for an enemy to fall completely to zero health before attempting to steal their skill. When an opponent flashes with an amber halo, they have entered an unbalance state. Activating your siphon button at this exact split second instantly finishes the enemy and refills a fraction of your stamina bar.

### 2. Manage the Dual Resource Gauges
Your survival relies on two interconnected bars:
- **Stamina**: Depleted by dodges, running, and basic weapon swings.
- **Focus Pips**: Built by landing clean light attacks and successful parries; spent to unleash stolen elite abilities.

Emptying your stamina bar incurs a 1.5-second recovery delay during which you cannot roll or block. Keeping at least 15% stamina in reserve ensures you can backstep away from unexpected wide-arc cleaves.
