## Core Combat: The Enemy Skill-Stealing System

The driving hook behind the title is its fluid, aggressive combat system centered on supernatural masks. You assume the role of an empty husk—formerly an inquisitive scholar—who claims a mythic artifact capable of siphoning martial techniques from defeated foes.

Unlike conventional RPGs where character progression relies on grinding experience points or upgrading numeric armor tiers, this title emphasizes active ability synthesis. By defeating adversaries scattered across forgotten provinces, you extract their signature attacks and slot them directly into custom combo strings.

### Strategic Skill Weaving

When facing hostile mobs or elite sentinels, combat moves at a rapid pace. Timing dodges, recognizing windups, and stringing stolen techniques together determine survival.

| Mechanic | In-Game Function | Tactical Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mask Absorption** | Harvests combat arts upon felling specific foes | Expands available moveset without stat gates |
| **Combo Chaining** | Weaves stolen abilities seamlessly into light/heavy strings | Staggers aggressive opponents and breaks guards |
| **Divine Powers** | Harnesses remnant elemental energy from fallen deities | Solves environmental puzzles and clears elite mobs |
| **Stamina & Evasion** | Tight, responsive dodge frames with low recovery delay | Bypasses wide-arc boss attacks and counterattacks |

As you venture further from the mountaintop kingdom of Wisdom into sprawling swamplands and derelict cathedrals, you face difficult choices. Narrative paths diverge depending on whether you pledge your blade to the fading deities of Life or align with the creeping influence of Death, unlocking alternate endings and unique combat encounters.
